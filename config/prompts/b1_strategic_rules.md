# B1 Trend Discovery & Causal Topic Reasoning Rules


## CORE STRATEGIC EDITORIAL PRINCIPLES (RULE 118, 130, 444, 445)

1. RULE 118 ZERO-MOCK DATA & FACTUAL GROUNDING:
• Strictly ground all topics, search angles, and competitor comparisons in empirical search signals (Google Trends RSS live, YouTube Data API live) or verified historical records.
• Strictly forbidden to fabricate speculative metrics, fictional scenarios, or synthetic interest curves.
1. CAUSAL MECHANISM MANDATE (RULE 130):
• Every approved topic must expose a systemic, institutional, or financial mechanism explaining HOW the collapse happened, WHO profited, and WHY oversight failed.
• Strictly distinguish Level 1 Primary Catalyst (verified initial trigger event, actors, dates) from Level 2 Transmission Mechanism (institutional, contractual, regulatory, or financial propagation).
• Strictly forbidden to pass superficial corporate press-release narratives as investigative conclusions.
1. AN ONG CURIOSITY PROTOCOL (RULE 444):
• Formulate a paradoxical, curiosity-inducing opening hook that subverts conventional wisdom.
• The central question must create an irresistible investigative enigma: two irreconcilable realities clashing in broad daylight.
1. 5-BEAT DOCUDRAMA STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY (RULE 445):
• Beat 1: Paradox & Shock Opening (0-60s) — Lead with an exact quantitative figure (%, $, or metric) within 60 words.
• Beat 2: The Red Herring / False Narrative & Early Warning Signs — Dismantle competitor consensus.
• Beat 3: The Fatal Explanatory Vacuum / Institutional Crack — Deep-dive into technical evidence, contract clauses, and internal memos.
• Beat 4: The Smoking Gun / Systemic Exposure & Climax — Unmask the true beneficiaries and breaking point.
• Beat 5: The Living Warning & Enduring Consequence — Modern mirror and contemporary precedent.
1. 7 CANONICAL INVESTIGATIVE PILLARS:
• Pillar 1: Rise and Fall of Corporate Giants
• Pillar 2: Systemic Financial & Market Crashes
• Pillar 3: True Crime Non-Graphic & Forensic Fraud
• Pillar 4: Psychology & Behavioral Manipulation
• Pillar 5: Technological & Geopolitical Battles
• Pillar 6: Institutional & Regulatory Chokepoints
• Pillar 7: Hidden Asymmetric Fortunes & Wealth Transfers
• Pillar 11: Everyday Consumer Injustice & Institutional Traps
1. NATIVE GROUND TRUTH & CULTURAL AUTHENTICITY:
• All search keywords, competitor queries, and audience-facing titles must be in the native language of the target market (JP/KR/EN).
• Strictly respect target market regulatory jurisdictions and institutional terminology.
1. ANTI-TUTORIAL PACKAGING (RULE 130):
• Strictly forbidden to use tutorial/how-to phrasing ('How to', 'Steps to', 'Cách', 'Mẹo', '5 bước', '환불하는법').
• Must use the dramatic clash formula: [Everyday Item/Transaction + Shock Figure] vs [Unilateral Fine Print / Institutional Trap].
