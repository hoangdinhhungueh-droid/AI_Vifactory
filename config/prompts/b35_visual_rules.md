# B3.5 Visual Prompt Forge & DACL Rules


## NOTION SOT CONCISE B3.5 VISUAL PRODUCTION RULES (RULES 116, 120, 122, 128, 435, 446)

1. 2-LAYER COMPOSITION SPECIFICATION (RULE 116):
• Layer 1 (Background 16:9 2K DCI 2560x1440): Wide-angle cinematic documentary environment, unobstructed bottom 20% safe-zone for subtitles, clear center-right negative space for foreground character overlay.
• Layer 2 (Foreground 1:1 2048x2048): Stylized editorial graphic novel digital illustration, waist-up three-quarter angle action framing with active hands manipulating physical documentary prop and directional gaze.
• Chroma Key Isolation: Foregrounds strictly isolated on solid #FF00FF pure magenta background.
1. EDITORIAL GRAPHIC NOVEL ART DIRECTION (RULE 435 & RULE 116):
• Rich graphic novel editorial illustration, bold inked linework, expressive cel-shading, rich cinematic color palette, high-contrast chiaroscuro lighting.
• Strictly forbidden: Photorealistic human photos, uncanny 3D renders, generic flat vector icons.
1. SUBTITLE SAFE-ZONE INTEGRITY (RULE 120):
• Bottom 20% vertical margin of Layer 1 Background strictly reserved for two-line word-level karaoke subtitles.
• Zero visual clutter, zero typewriter labels, zero critical evidentiary elements in the bottom 20% safe-zone.
1. CINEMATIC CONTINUITY & ERA AUTHENTICITY (RULE 128):
• Harmonize lighting angle, color palette, and atmospheric mood between paired Layer 1 & Layer 2 assets.
• Zero domain bleed: never inject modern digital devices into historical periods or rural relics into high-tech corporate environments.
1. INDIGENOUS CULTURAL PROPS & ANTI-ENGLISH BLEED (RULE 122 & RULE 128):
• Every scene MUST include tangible local cultural artifacts:
• JP: Hanko cinnabar red seals, King File spine tabs, sha-sho corporate lapel pins, Japanese police identification badges.
• KR: Official round seals/stamps (ingam), sawonjeung corporate ID lanyards, Taegeuk court crests, Chaebol executive folders.
• STRICTLY FORBIDDEN: English/Latin text or English words on whiteboards, walls, signs, documents, or folders for Asian markets (JP/KR).
1. ENGLISH PRODUCTION BRIDGE (RULE 446):
• All visual prompt descriptions, negative prompts, and art direction parameters must be written in descriptive, vivid English for image generation backends.
1. MARKET ETHNICITY & REGIONAL ARCHITECTURE CONFORMANCE (RULE 122 & RULE 128):
• Foreground characters MUST feature authentic ethnic phenotype (bone structure, eye shape, styling, attire), dynamic high-stakes body posture (leaning forward, decisive pointing, clenched fist), and intense facial micro-expressions (furrowed brow, sharp intense glare, zero dead eyes/blank stare). ZERO Caucasian/Western bias in Asian markets.
• Backgrounds MUST feature authentic local corporate/urban architecture matching target market geography. ZERO generic American cubicles or Western skyline mismatch.
