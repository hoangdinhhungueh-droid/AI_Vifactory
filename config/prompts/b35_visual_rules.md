# B3.5 Visual Prompt Forge & DACL Rules

## NOTION SOT CONCISE B3.5 VISUAL PRODUCTION RULES (RULES 116, 120, 122, 128, 435, 446)

1. 2-LAYER COMPOSITION (RULE 116):
• Layer 1 (Background 16:9): Cinematic documentary environment, unobstructed bottom 20% safe-zone for subtitles, clear right negative space for character overlay.
• Layer 2 (Foreground 1:1): Stylized editorial graphic novel illustration, waist-up framing with active hands manipulating physical documentary prop.
• Chroma Key Isolation: Foregrounds strictly isolated on solid #FF00FF pure magenta background.

2. EDITORIAL GRAPHIC NOVEL ART DIRECTION (RULE 435 & RULE 116):
• Rich graphic novel editorial illustration, bold inked linework, expressive cel-shading, high-contrast chiaroscuro lighting.
• Strictly forbidden: Photorealistic photos, uncanny 3D renders, generic flat vector icons.

3. SUBTITLE SAFE-ZONE INTEGRITY (RULE 120):
• Bottom 20% vertical margin of Layer 1 Background strictly reserved for word-level karaoke subtitles.
• Zero visual clutter or critical evidence in the bottom 20% safe-zone.

4. CINEMATIC CONTINUITY & ERA AUTHENTICITY (RULE 128):
• Harmonize lighting angle, color palette, and mood between paired Layer 1 & Layer 2 assets.
• Zero domain bleed: never inject modern devices into historical periods or Western cues into Asian scenes.

5. INDIGENOUS CULTURAL PROPS & ANTI-ENGLISH BLEED (RULE 122 & RULE 128):
• JP: Hanko red seals, King File spine tabs, sha-sho corporate lapel pins, Japanese police badges.
• KR: Round stamps (ingam), sawonjeung corporate ID lanyards, Taegeuk crests, Chaebol folders.
• Strictly forbidden: English/Latin text on whiteboards, signs, or documents for Asian markets (JP/KR).

6. ENGLISH PRODUCTION BRIDGE (RULE 446):
• All prompt descriptions and art direction parameters must be in descriptive English for image generation backends.

7. MARKET ETHNICITY & ARCHITECTURE CONFORMANCE (RULE 122 & RULE 128):
• Foreground characters: authentic ethnic phenotype, dynamic posture, intense micro-expressions (zero dead eyes).
• Backgrounds: authentic local corporate/urban architecture matching target market geography.
